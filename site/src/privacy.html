This Privacy Policy explains how Mikael Vesavuori ("we," "us," or "Kod") handles personal data when you visit the Kod website or use the Kod software.

The short version: Kod is self-hosted software. Your repositories, workflows, secrets, and operational data stay on your infrastructure. The website does not include analytics, payment scripts, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking.

1. Who We Are

Data Controller: Mikael Vesavuori
Email: contact@itskod.com
Location: Göteborg, Sweden

2. Website Data

The Kod website is served as static files. We do not run website analytics, payment widgets, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or cookie-based behavioral tracking.

Basic technical request information may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the site, maintain security, and operate the network. We do not use this information to profile visitors.

3. Self-Hosted Kod Data

When you run Kod, we do not receive your operational data. We do not collect:

  • Repositories, commits, branches, or Git content
  • Workflow files, workflow logs, or workflow environment data
  • Secrets, tokens, collaborator data, or configuration values
  • Telemetry, feature usage, performance metrics, or phone-home data

4. Support Communications

If you contact us directly, we process the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message content, and technical details included in the conversation.

We use that information only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the project.

5. Legal Basis

Under GDPR, we process support communications based on consent or legitimate interests. We process minimal technical hosting data based on legitimate interests in delivering and securing the website.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with advertisers or behavioral marketing platforms. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical request data as necessary to serve the website.

7. Retention

  • Support communications: retained for up to 1 year after resolution unless a longer period is needed for legal or security reasons.
  • Technical hosting data: retained according to the hosting provider's operational and security practices.

8. Your Rights

As an individual in the EU/EEA, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to, or request portability of your personal data. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise your rights: Email contact@itskod.com with your request.

9. Security

We keep the website simple and minimize data collection. For your own Kod deployment, you are responsible for securing your infrastructure, access controls, backups, and network exposure.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the project changes. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a new date.
